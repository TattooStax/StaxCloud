# Icon Sheet Candidates — American Traditional Flash (for owner approval)

Two generated candidate sheets, 12 emblems each (4×3 grid), same subjects, same brief:
reaper (mascot) · horseshoe magnet · rubber stamp · speech bubble · envelope · clipboard ·
open book · contacts (two busts) · tattoo machine · framed rose · toolbox · chain links.

These are candidates only — nothing is wired into the app. The owner approves a sheet first;
the winner gets cut into tiles afterwards.

## Sheet A — sheet-a.png (2,737,702 bytes)

Reads clearly at 48px: reaper (dusty-pink hood, teal scythe blade — the strongest mascot of the
two, and the teal blade ties it to the product's teal), envelope, clipboard, open book, framed
rose, toolbox, chain links. All bold silhouettes with thick outlines.

Weaker cells:
- Speech bubble: rendered as a cream ribbon banner with teal tails — beautiful, but reads as
  "banner" more than "speech bubble" at small size.
- Tattoo machine: the richest drawing on the sheet (gold coil machine), but the coil/screw
  detail is the most likely to soften at 48px.
- Contacts: two profile busts, one in a red cap — reads fine, faces carry a little more detail
  than ideal.
- Magnet carries tiny + / − tip marks (symbols, not lettering).

Palette: disciplined — teal, dusty pink/red, mustard gold, cream on near-black, matching the
reference family. Slight vintage distress texture on the background. No text, no letters,
no numbers anywhere.

## Sheet B — sheet-b.png (2,304,752 bytes)

Reads clearly at 48px: reaper (gold scythe), magnet, envelope, framed rose, toolbox, and two
standouts — the contacts emblem is pure cream/teal silhouette busts (the most small-size-proof
cell on either sheet), and the speech bubble is a true teal bubble crossed by a cream banner,
the more literal "Channels" read.

Weaker cells:
- Rubber stamp: flatter and more abstract than A's; at 48px it could read as a generic block.
- Chain links: the red link carries a soft gradient fade instead of a flat fill — the one real
  palette-discipline slip on either sheet.
- Clipboard and open book use busier black line-work inside; fine at tile size, slightly noisy.
- Envelope is plain cream with no color accent — clear but the dullest cell.

Palette: same limited set, consistently applied except the chain-link gradient. Speckled
charcoal background, a touch grainier than A. No text or lettering.

## Pick: Sheet A

A is the stronger sheet overall: the better reaper (and the brand mascot is the cell that
matters most), the better stamp, machine, envelope, and clipboard, and zero palette slips.
B wins two individual cells — contacts and speech bubble — so if the owner likes A overall,
those two B cells are the ones worth a redraw in A's hand when the sheet is cut into tiles.
